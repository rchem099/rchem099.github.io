01 · AI / FULL-STACK · FALL 2026

AI Voice News Assistant

Developed a full-stack voice-enabled news assistant that transcribes spoken questions, generates source-linked answers using a local language model, and reads responses aloud. Integrated faster-whisper for speech recognition, Ollama for local LLM inference, and browser speech synthesis. Built an RSS aggregation pipeline covering multiple international news publishers, with article deduplication and PostgreSQL storage through Supabase. Implemented authenticated REST APIs, user-specific data access, listening progress tracking, and consent-based interest memory with voice commands for preference management. Instrumented transcription, database queries, news retrieval, and model inference to identify latency bottlenecks. Configured a temporary HTTPS demonstration using Waitress and Cloudflare Tunnel.
